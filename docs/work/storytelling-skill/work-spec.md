## Prior rewrites and boundaries

| Bad default | Replacement instinct | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A theme slogan plus unrelated plot/character/dialogue | Build one causal exchange between desire, governing philosophy, action, consequences, and value | Not every literary work requires a thesis or transformation |
| Obstacles accumulated without consequential progression | Later difficulties arise from or expose earlier attempts and change the cost of choice | Ordinary execution and cooperation can also carry meaning |
| Character biography, trauma, and quirks | Design particular attachments, contradictions, capacities, relationships, and revealing conduct | Do not invent pathology or require an arc for every character |
| Dialogue as information or polished thematic speeches | Specific speech acts under foreground/background desires, with consequential reception | Candor, pleasure, and companionship need not conceal manipulation |
| The hero chooses the lesson and wins | Separate belief, capacity, decision, action, result, and final value charge | Prepared help, distributed agency, tragedy, and grace remain available |
| One fixed genre taxonomy dictates every story | Infer or preserve the project's audience contract and governing value | A user may explicitly choose a framework; archived legacy instructions are not a second runtime authority |
| Every subplot confirms the lesson | Independent value trajectories may contradict or qualify the central claim | Do not manufacture contradiction where the work seeks simplicity |
| Story outline treated as finished art | Voice, focalization, rhythm, embodiment, and time create the reader's actual encounter | Preserve the user's requested medium and scope |
| Large skill means load everything | Small router and substantial selected references | Load more when the task truly crosses domains |
