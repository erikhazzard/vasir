## Authorship and application decisions

All fiction samples in these four modules are newly written for the skill. They include the train tickets, stored letters, anxious baby-holder, piano syntax comparison, snow-clearing neighbors, confidential farewell invitation, Ivo/Dara soup ending, and bell-city scenes. They are not quotations from the books or copies of the canonical synthesis's examples.

The strong default remains causal pursuit through consequential choices and progressive complications. The form module also gives positive, operational alternatives for observational, associative, ritual, serial-reset, contemplative, comic, and minimal-conflict work. This follows explicit source exceptions and the synthesis's broader narrative scope without pretending every art form secretly satisfies the same scene formula.

The narration module distinguishes access, diction, temporal position, address, interpretation, and reliability. It treats physiological and absolute reader-response claims in the craft essays as unsupported generalizations; the usable techniques concern specific choices in language and attention. Thought-verb deletion, pronoun avoidance, perpetual physical business, and mandatory shortness are exercises or options, not permanent rules.

The world module uses material/institutional transactions, competing cultural participation, mythic questions, succession, and maintenance. Campbell's analogical and metaphysical system is a generative lens rather than universal anthropology. Estés is credited separately. Wider scale does not automatically prove consent, moral rightness, or repair.

The revision module uses observational evidence, scope diagnosis, matched prototypes, contrasting interpretations, narrow changes, and dependency repair. Its tests are editorial comparisons, not algorithms or experimental proof. The final read must assess the actual encounter and preserve strengths the chosen diagnostic cannot represent.

No old storytelling skill is used as a live authority or routed dependency. References point only to sibling files in the new master package. Source lineage footers preserve the distinction between primary passages, inherited synthesis, and new operational examples.
