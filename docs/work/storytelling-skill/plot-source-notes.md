## Lineage and synthesis decisions

The value/cause formulation, expectation–result gap, crisis versus climax, antagonism field, and behavioral beat come chiefly from McKee. Coyne supplies especially useful inventory and reversibility operations, within a shared intellectual lineage; he is not counted as independent empirical confirmation. Bird supplies organic setup and explicit permission for different degrees of thematic settlement.

The local canonical analyses and synthesis develop several important interpretations: corrupted means of correction, success creating dependence, situated and distributed agency, counterworld testing, values without a shared conversion rate, and honest distinctions between events, discoveries, and judgments. The skill preserves those as useful craft syntheses rather than retroactively attributing all of them to the original authors.

New operationalizations include tracing causal/motivational/practical/audience dependencies separately; pairing candidate controlling ideas with the climactic action they demand; distinguishing contrary subplot outcomes from actual causal contradiction; auditing thought/intention/action/result; and giving each operation a concrete diagnosis or repair.
