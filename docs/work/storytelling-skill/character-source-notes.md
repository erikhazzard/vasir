## Main synthesis decisions

1. **A character is an encountered pattern, not a completed dossier.** Biography, wounds, traits, and secrets become useful when they alter perception, behavior, or the selected experience. Work, ordinary pleasure, and shared activity can make a person compelling without a corrective ordeal.
2. **Keep concepts distinct.** Value, value charge, object of desire, deeper satisfaction, philosophy, strategy, personality, immediate intent, and background desire generate different questions and different revisions.
3. **Preserve the limits of BCI.** Believe is not testimony, Care is not approval, and Invest is not a synonym for all narrative interest. Name alternate invitations directly.
4. **Qualify pressure with actual agency.** More danger does not automatically reveal a deeper preference. Knowledge, resources, authority, capacity, and costs to other people change what an act establishes.
5. **Make a flaw's mechanism operational.** Wrong domain, excessive intensity, harmful target, wrong priority, and changed conditions call for different changes. Not every impairment or harmful condition needs a redeeming gift.
6. **Keep the cast reciprocal.** Supporting people have independent futures. A request, refusal, gift, or reconciliation changes their options as well as the protagonist's.
7. **Separate recognition, conduct, and outcome.** Insight does not automatically produce skill, restore trust, remove barriers, or earn success. Stable, corrupting, disillusioned, exhausted, and delayed-insight trajectories remain available.
8. **Treat dialogue as part of the causal event.** Intention, reception, and effect can diverge. Actual responses modify the next action. Candor can carry subtext; shared pleasure need not be covert warfare.
9. **Preserve primary-source qualifications.** McKee's minimal-conflict scene, Bird's à-la-carte tools and exceptions, and the tension between pressured choice and free choice prevent universalized prescriptions.
