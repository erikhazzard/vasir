## Why the previous Writing leaderboard was empty

The old index required every model to have complete official totals on every source with any available total. Plot twists had two eligible settings (Sol and Luna); Dungeon Master had only Astra. Their intersection was empty. Requiring both Plot twists and Magic within Storytelling additionally hid 31 of Magic's 33 complete model results.

The new comparison policy is `planned-roster-finalized-equal-subcategory-complete-paired-index-v2`:

1. Among finalized publications with at least one complete official paired model total, choose the widest **planned configuration-identity roster**. Break equal-size ties by ascending benchmark ID. If none is ready, retain a planned roster for disclosure only.
2. Include finalized official sources covering every identity in that roster. A wider unfinished or wholly unscored publication cannot erase existing results. Identity sets, not just roster counts, must match.
3. Weight included subcategories equally and included benchmarks equally within each subcategory. Every ranked model uses the same source set and weights under both conditions. Rank only complete paired totals; never average whichever tests happen to exist for an individual model.
4. Preserve each source's own cases, repetitions, panels, exclusions, raw results, and reports. Provisional scores cannot establish readiness or enter this comparison. Narrower studies stay visible in the benchmark breakdown.

Currently the basis is **First discovery of magic: 33 model settings, three trials, two judge models crossed with two review contexts**. Its scores and ranks are unchanged. Storytelling is the only included segment, at 100%; an unmeasured Dungeon Master segment is not drawn. The page names this exact basis and remains excluded from Overall. It is not a calibrated general-writing ranking.
