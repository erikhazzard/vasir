# Ash & Echo implementation seam

Self-contained static ES modules. World width is 420 logical units; y increases downward. Camera uses `game.cameraY` as the world-space top of the viewport. Runtime viewport height is supplied by `game.resize(logicalHeight)`.

`game.js` exports `createGame()`. Returned game exposes `width`, `height`, `viewportHeight`, `player` (`x,y,w,h,vx,vy,grounded,wallDir,airJumps,facing,motion`), `platforms` (`id,x,y,w,h,kind`, with optional `suspension`, `solid`, `route`, `landmark`, `structure`, `support` and `resonance`), `hazards` (`x,y,w,h,orientation`), `checkpoint` (`x,y,w,h,active`), `goal` (`x,y,w,h`), `cameraY`, `time`, `elapsed`, `deaths`, `maxAltitude`, `respawnTimer`, `state` (`ready`,`playing`,`won`), and `events`.

Methods: `resize(height)`, `start()`, `restart()`, `step(dt, input)`, `drainEvents()`. Input: `{axis:-1..1,jumpPressed:boolean,jumpHeld:boolean}`. `jumpPressed` is one press edge, consumed once. Events: `{type,x,y,vx,vy,intensity}`; types `jump`, `doubleJump`, `wallJump`, `land`, `death`, `respawn`, `checkpoint`, `win`. A `land` event additionally identifies `platformId`, `suspended` (boolean), optional `landmark`, normalized `contactOffset`, `fallDistance` (logical feet-to-surface distance from the latest apex), `impactSpeed` (positive vertical speed before collision stops the player), `landingSeverity` (continuous 0–1 presentation weight), and optional `resonance` pivot metadata. Ground `jump` events carry the departing platform’s landmark when present. Death additionally records `cause` (`thorns` or `fall`), `hazardIndex` (-1 without a hazard), and the world-space `contactX,contactY` for causal feedback. `step` uses seconds; main passes fixed 1/120 steps. Larger calls subdivide into steps no larger than 1/120; zero time does nothing. World collisions and level belong to game.js.

Free-hanging ledges have `suspension: {homeX,homeY,x,y,vx,vy}`; its `x,y` are offsets from the authored home position. Side masonry, checkpoint and summit contacts remain fixed. Simulation advances each spring before player movement and writes the live collider coordinates to `platform.x,y`. Offset bounds are ±3.5 horizontally and −4 to +11 vertically, with a loaded vertical equilibrium of +4.2. A grounded rider inherits the supporting ledge's displacement, keeping feet on its live surface. Horizontal carry resolves through the same solid collision as input movement, so sway cannot push a rider inside a wall; spring velocity does not alter the authored jump impulse. Landing severity scales the stone impulse; takeoff releases it without repeated landing events while it settles. Leaving support releases the rider through the normal coyote/air-jump rules. Death clears grounded contact; springs settle during the retry delay. Restart clears support and spring state and restores every home position.

Landing severity continues increasing with descent distance after the 900-unit/s terminal speed, and is attenuated by pre-contact speed so wall slides dissipate force. Ground, wall and air jumps start a new apex; standing, respawn and restart clear the previous descent. Current mapping is `clamp((fallDistance−8)/500,0,1)^.72 × clamp(impactSpeed/900,0,1)^1.2`. Legacy `intensity` remains available, while body, surface ash, rigging/cages/bells, contact camera spring, loose-air ash and audio share severity. No landing delay changes physics or input. A queued ground jump can follow `land` in the same authoritative step and immediately cancel the body pose and contact audio tails. Fixed fog and aperture lighting do not react to landing.

Four projecting masonry ribs append six `wall` rectangles each after the original course and outer walls. Each segment carries a shared `rib` descriptor (`id,x,y,w,h,segments`) and its `ribSegment` index. Descriptor segments contain plain rectangle geometry; there are no circular references. Simulation collides with every segment normally. Rendering draws segment zero's complete tapered silhouette once and skips the remaining segments. The first four original ledges are replaced by six named opening contacts: `first-hop`, `second-hop`, `wall-transfer`, `crossing-recovery`, `resonant-stone`, and `opening-refuge`. The existing ascent retains `ledge-5` through `ledge-28`; hazards and the later checkpoint/summit remain in place. The initial plinth is solid through its floor connection; other ledges retain top-only contact. The opening refuge is a fixed rest, without checkpoint state. Its alternative lower recovery route remains valid.

Camera snaps initially to the 64% composition anchor, then follows only outside a 54–78% vertical band. Nearby landings keep the frame steady; immediate 17–84% visibility safeguards and world bounds catch long falls. The fixed-step camera is deterministic and does not gate input.
