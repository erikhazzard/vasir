# Baseline v13 — rejected for new request

Scoped verdict: DOES NOT SLAP for the requested radically different double jump and smoke/ash trail. This does not reopen user-accepted world contact effects or claim global game quality.

Material: native 390×844 Chrome emulation, ordinary keyboard inputs and RAF, three repeated first/second jump pairs, releases and wall kicks. Starting positions arranged; no frame stepping and no screenshots inside action windows. The video was extracted at 25 fps. Source hashes and event times are in receipt.json; production sources archived before changes.

I am a cream-eyed ash creature climbing ruined masonry. I should jump between shelves and away from hazardous edges. The character remains readable and the next platform remains clear. When I press the second jump, its direction is renewed but its visual action reads almost identically to the first jump. I would keep playing the platformer, but this event does not yet supply the new transformation toy the user requested.

Three perceptual misses:
1. Second input only produces a short flattened-head beat, then the same narrow comet. Without knowing the input, it reads as continuous ascent.
2. Long connected black strings dominate the silhouette. Detached flecks do not counteract the head-and-flagellum read.
3. Left-behind matter remains narrow and weak; the spent-jump location never holds a broad smoky volume that drifts, grows and dissipates.

Acceptance target: a visible topology change, substantial spatial separation between reborn head and spent body, an independently evolving ash cloud with detached flakes, repeated coherently at ordinary pace. Keep cream-eyed identity and hazard clarity; avoid rings, neon wings, or red damage semantics. No S/10x/human-acceptance claim is warranted by agent review.
