## Two remaining changes with the most value

1. **High — Give the summit its own airspace.** Compare `route/land-14-3s.png` with `route/land-26-3s.png`: the summit largely repeats the refuge’s gray backlight, vertical cathedral filling, dark portal, and suspended ledges. Make the upper roughly 550 world pixels emerge above a visible fog ceiling. Open pale warm-gray sky immediately behind the final gate, approximately x 110–310 / y 40–310; reduce distant architecture contrast inside that opening; retain darker ruined framing at its sides. Keep a denser rolling cloud bank below y 500–650 so the lower climb visibly falls beneath the clouds. The intended read is enclosed stone, crossing fog, then a small dark gate against clear light. A uniform brightness increase would lose that composition.
2. **Polish — Make the spider’s response legible from its new perch.** The earlier ordinary route sailed above a mostly cropped black body. The relocation fixes that staging: in `new-spider-arranged-1s.png`, its head and front legs now extend into the light immediately beside the player. Across the held-near 1 s / 3 s views, pose changes remain subtle. If the root’s new actual approach confirms that, use a brief, larger inward head recoil and one clearly lifted front leg, followed by a slower return. Preserve its wall grip and dim distant eyes. The observable requirement is one readable near/far change at native phone size, not a brighter creature everywhere.

The prior ash repair is now resolved in my refreshed observation: `new-ash-open.png` shows separated fragments around a small eye core, and `new-ash-reform.png` restores the body. The continuous bat-wing silhouette is gone.

## Addendum — final summit composition, images only

Compared `../scenes/phone-26.png` and `../scenes/retina-26.png` with `../summit-final/phone-26.png` and `../summit-final/retina-26.png`. No browser was opened for this follow-up.

**The new framing is an improvement worth retaining.** The asymmetric middle-distance buttress supplies a clear, darker structural plane around the goal. Its rising curve gives this scene more deliberate direction, and the pale space behind the small dark gate separates the destination more strongly. Both changes survive native phone size; they are not benefits visible only in the larger image. The scene has more architectural depth and a more composed arrival than the earlier, uniformly misty summit.

The earlier environmental-arrival concern is **partly addressed**, with two remaining artistic limits:

- The dark arch is the most conspicuous change. It makes the summit read as a distinctive upper chamber under a stone roof. The transition into clearer air above a cloud ceiling remains restrained: distant cathedral detail still fills the central view, and the lower cloud bank does not establish a strong visible boundary in these stills. A claim that the player now emerges above the clouds would need a fresh moving ascent and a more evident change in the lower-versus-upper air.
- The buttress's inner edge passes immediately behind the portal crown, reducing the clean light around that small silhouette. About 12–20 native phone pixels of clearance above the crown would give the gate more breathing room while preserving the asymmetric framing. This is the smallest remaining composition refinement; another prominent architectural element would compete with the destination.

These are image-based composition observations. This follow-up does not establish rolling-cloud motion, the reveal during ascent, new performance behavior, or a higher whole-game grade. The previous play evidence and its limits remain in force.
