The active-play visual verdict is **SLAPS**. I am the small soot creature; I should climb the nearest carved ledge and continue toward the light. The precarious descent and distant shrine make the climb worth following. Acting produces a clear change in trajectory, silhouette, contact dust, and height. Landing restores a stable pose before another launch. My biggest visual confusion is limited to the decorative versus structural appearance of a few repeated floating corbels; it did not hide their usable top edges. I would continue playing from the observed moments.

This verdict comes from valid active material: [desktop play](desktop-01-active.png), native mobile frames, before/input/after touch sequences `native-00` through `native-11`, a real route [recording](review-playthrough.webm), and state/event receipts. I visually inspected the frame sequences; I do not claim to have listened to the recording. Native screenshot state records were read after capture completion and can be later than the exact visible frame. `native-04` and `native-05` show a second ground launch/landing; the separately verified `native-10` and `double-touch.json` establish the actual air double jump.

The adaptive keyboard route reached grounded success in **41.583 simulated seconds**, including one intentional checkpoint death and one missed upper landing recovered through ordinary movement. Logged events include 35 jumps, 34 double jumps, 34 landings, one wall jump, checkpoint, death, respawn, and win. A tap on the actual restart button returned to a grounded start with zero deaths and an inactive checkpoint. Neither route nor touch probes teleported the character or called a debug input setter. The route harness recomputes the next ledge from the actual grounded foot position after recovery, avoiding stale-target stalls.

The route reported no page errors. Its screenshot/video-heavy capture produced timing stalls, so it is not clean performance evidence. My separate ten-second wall-contact sample at DPR2, with screenshots and video capture absent, retained **590 frames: p95 16.7 ms, p99/max 16.8 ms, zero over 33.4 ms**. This is a credible local runtime sanity check, not a phone or long-duration thermal qualification. Touch controls measured 64×64 CSS pixels for each direction and 82×82 for jump; the native frames keep these controls beneath the principal action.

There are **no hard visual blockers**. The strongest reason the sequence works is that atmosphere frames each next landing while the expressive black creature supplies the motion focal point. The highest-value optional polish would be two or three irregular broken-end variants for floating corbels, preserving every collision edge and their current value separation. This is not required before the user plays this reference build.
