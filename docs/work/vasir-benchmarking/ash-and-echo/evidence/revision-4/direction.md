## The actual problem

The scene reads as individually detailed objects assembled over a softly enlarged backdrop. Its Gothic subject is consistent, but its **material, edge, structural and value languages are not**. The thin corbels and bright carved gateway ask us to inspect ornament. The hero asks us to read an expressive charcoal silhouette. The architecture behind them asks us to accept blur as depth. These competing treatments never become one convincing place.

The supplied LIMBO image establishes a craft principle: large black playable masses, readable graphite structures crossing and enclosing the space, pale air confined by those structures, and progressively softer distance. Its structure still works when small detail is ignored. Ash & Echo currently relies much more on detail and fog. Copying the reference's exact scenery or characters is unnecessary; its mass/value/focus discipline is the useful target.

The new direction can address this root problem. **Near-black objects against the same mint-gray plate would not.** The new kit only helps if it enables complete structural silhouettes, consistent charcoal materials, and bold middle-distance occlusion. Four replacement objects do not establish a visual system by themselves.

## Calibration amendment proposed for the canonical judge

The previous reviewer correctly detected relative depth improvement and readable first actions. It then promoted **“no reproduced gameplay obstruction” into “no mandatory further environmental repair.”** The same report explicitly called a visible reference gap “not a newly demonstrated play blocker.” That is the central category mistake: this task's acceptance bar includes authored visual quality. An obvious assembly defect or weak mass composition does not need to prevent a jump to require repair.

The previous final captures themselves retain square-ended cropped corbels and pale middle architecture. The reviewer even inspected checkpoint/upper-route material, where the shrine's competing material is visible. Some of the failure was therefore **judgment**, not missing evidence. The narrow final cached-lighting preservation check could establish that appearance did not regress; it could not validate the underlying art direction. Native DPR1 presentation also understated the user's enlarged raster complaint. “Fresh model” reduced shared narrative exposure but did not remove the model's common tendency to reward completion, detail and incremental improvement.

Suggested judge insertion:

> Treat latest human reference-linked visual ratings as the active anchor. “Improved over C” and “no play blocker” do not establish readiness above C. Judge the current active frame against the reference's mass composition, structural integrity, material coherence and resolution at both native mobile and the user's actual desktop raster scale. Exposed crop boundaries, detached decorative supports, inconsistent object materials, or an atmospheric treatment that collapses the middle distance are required visual repairs when plainly visible, even if traversal remains readable. A local closure says only which finding closed. Do not infer a higher letter grade or “no required repair” from a complete route, feature count, generated-asset detail, layer count, or the absence of newly reproduced bugs.

For the next review, require three direct answers before any recommendation:

1. **What is the strongest remaining C-quality visual feature?** Name the frame and the visible form. If there is none, explain which reference-level quality now replaces each of the human's concrete complaints.
2. **Do I believe these objects belong to one physical place before I notice their detail?** Specifically inspect one free platform, one wall joint/chain, the middle crossing, and the portal at both scales.
3. **Would the same composition still be strong with the texture mentally removed?** If the answer depends on the ornate asset, fog glow or explanatory prose, the art direction has not met the target.

An honest C/C+ diagnosis can coexist with meaningful repairs. A coherent but visibly ordinary candidate may deserve a bounded B opinion. Neither merits a higher letter until the reference-level qualities are actually visible across the sampled climb. Preserve the earlier optimistic review as a calibration miss; do not rewrite it into a successful quality gate after the fact.
