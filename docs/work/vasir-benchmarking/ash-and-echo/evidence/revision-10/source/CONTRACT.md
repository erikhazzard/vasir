# Ash & Echo implementation seam

Self-contained static ES modules. World width is 420 logical units; y increases downward. Camera uses `game.cameraY` as the world-space top of the viewport. Runtime viewport height is supplied by `game.resize(logicalHeight)`.

`game.js` exports `createGame()`. Returned game exposes `width`, `height`, `viewportHeight`, `player` (`x,y,w,h,vx,vy,grounded,wallDir,airJumps,facing,motion`), `platforms` (`id,x,y,w,h,kind`, with optional `suspension`, `solid`, `route`, `landmark`, `structure`, `support` and `resonance`), `hazards` (`x,y,w,h,orientation`), `checkpoint` (`x,y,w,h,active`), `goal` (`x,y,w,h`), `cameraY`, `time`, `elapsed`, `deaths`, `maxAltitude`, `respawnTimer`, `state` (`ready`,`playing`,`won`), and `events`.

Methods: `resize(height)`, `start()`, `restart()`, `step(dt, input)`, `drainEvents()`. Input: `{axis:-1..1,jumpPressed:boolean,jumpHeld:boolean}`. `jumpPressed` is one press edge, consumed once. Events: `{type,x,y,vx,vy,intensity}`; types `jump`, `doubleJump`, `wallJump`, `land`, `death`, `respawn`, `checkpoint`, `win`. A `land` event additionally identifies `platformId`, `suspended` (boolean), optional `landmark`, normalized `contactOffset`, and optional `resonance` pivot metadata. Ground `jump` events carry the departing platform’s landmark when present. Death additionally records `cause` (`thorns` or `fall`), `hazardIndex` (-1 without a hazard), and the world-space `contactX,contactY` for causal feedback. `step` uses seconds; main passes fixed 1/120 steps. Larger calls subdivide into steps no larger than 1/120; zero time does nothing. World collisions and level belong to game.js.

Free-hanging ledges have `suspension: {homeX,homeY,x,y,vx,vy}`; its `x,y` are offsets from the authored home position. Side masonry, checkpoint and summit contacts remain fixed. Simulation advances each spring before player movement and writes the live collider coordinates to `platform.x,y`. Offset bounds are ±3.5 horizontally and −4 to +11 vertically, with a loaded vertical equilibrium of +4.2. A grounded rider inherits the supporting ledge's displacement, keeping feet on its live surface; spring velocity does not alter the authored jump impulse. Landing and takeoff apply impulses to the stone without repeated landing events while it settles. Leaving support releases the rider through the normal coyote/air-jump rules. Death clears grounded contact; springs settle during the retry delay. Restart clears support and spring state and restores every home position.

Four projecting masonry ribs append six `wall` rectangles each after the original course and outer walls. Each segment carries a shared `rib` descriptor (`id,x,y,w,h,segments`) and its `ribSegment` index. Descriptor segments contain plain rectangle geometry; there are no circular references. Simulation collides with every segment normally. Rendering draws segment zero's complete tapered silhouette once and skips the remaining segments. The first four original ledges are replaced by six named opening contacts: `first-hop`, `second-hop`, `wall-transfer`, `crossing-recovery`, `resonant-stone`, and `opening-refuge`. The existing ascent retains `ledge-5` through `ledge-28`; hazards and the later checkpoint/summit remain in place. The initial plinth is solid through its floor connection; other ledges retain top-only contact. The opening refuge is a fixed rest, without checkpoint state. Its alternative lower recovery route remains valid.

Camera snaps initially to the 64% composition anchor, then follows only outside a 54–78% vertical band. Nearby landings keep the frame steady; immediate 17–84% visibility safeguards and world bounds catch long falls. The fixed-step camera is deterministic and does not gate input.

`renderer.js` exports `createRenderer(canvas, assets)`. Renderer exposes `reset()`, `resize(cssWidth, cssHeight, dpr)`, `render(game, dt)`, `emit(events)`, and `setReducedMotion(boolean)`. Main loads ten image keys: `background`, `stone`, `hero`, `spider`, `charcoal-architecture`, `charcoal-buttress`, `charcoal-belfry`, `charcoal-refuge`, `charcoal-bell`, and `charcoal-vertical-facade`. `background` loads `assets/background-birdless.png`; other keys use matching filenames. The spider is optional; assets may initially be absent. Earlier `background.png`, `midground`, `corbel`, `shrine`, `depth-arch` and `charcoal-arcade` images remain as source art but are no longer downloaded.

Renderer owns world compositing, complete platform silhouettes, continuous wall caches, cached iron rigging/thorns, particles, and a bounded contact spring for camera displacement. `lighting.js` owns five fixed cathedral apertures and their shared finite light field. The same field shades the existing cached stone faces, precomputes selected resting iron-link highlights, samples moving cable segments and ash, and supplies two visible opening uniforms to the existing middle fog submission. Nearer architecture is composited after that lit air. The chipped aperture reveals are painted into existing wall caches. Stone and resting iron exposure are baked at their authored home position; their small suspension displacement transports that material without per-frame pixel repainting. No new GPU target, texture, sampler or frame owner is introduced. Platforms draw at their live collider coordinates. Outer wall caches include eight logical pixels of finished coverage beyond the screen-facing edges and lower extent; visible source slices include the same margin above/below the viewport. This covers the shared ±5-pixel impact transform without moving inner collision faces. Extending only transparent cache bounds or leaving the outer strip unshaded would reintroduce the bright/gray kick borders. Cached rigging transforms from fixed wall pins to the moving stone sockets; paths and platform rasters are not rebuilt each frame. Each cable caches eight segment pairs and a full-length resting path pair. Suspended landing events activate a traveling/reflected contact response and slower opposing span recoil for 1.4 seconds, with bow capped at25 logical pixels, with delayed grit at the wall pins after 170ms. Resting and reduced-motion cables use the full-length pair; reset expires pending responses. Both endpoints follow the fixed pin and live socket throughout. Hanging cages attach to painted undersides sampled once when each platform cache is built. Cages and separately cached bell bodies/clappers react to nearby action events; peripheral spiders use cached limb cutouts, local regrips and player-directed eyes. The opening stone owns one additional attached bell that follows its suspension: chains/pin grit respond at170ms, bell angular force at220ms and the corresponding sound at350ms. Low approach contacts use quieter material response and character recoil; the opening refuge catches a static ivory spill baked into its platform cache. These prop springs and poses are presentation state. The read-only `sceneryState` getter returns copied `cages`, `rigging`, `bells` and `spiders` arrays for inspection; each `rigging` entry exposes `platformId`, `age`, `strength`, `segments`, `leftBow` and `rightBow`. The getter allocates only when read. `reset()` clears transient effects and reactive prop state and forwards reset to character and depth. Hazards render after mist and character effects so danger remains legible. Rendering never mutates simulation.

`depth.js` exports `createDepth(assets, atmosphere)`, with `resize(height)`, `drawBehind(context, game, clock, reducedMotion)`, `drawNear(context, game, reducedMotion)`, `emit(events)` and `reset()`. It prepares tone/focus once per source image, then draws distant city spires, a continuous vertical facade and belfries, middle piers, three moving mist layers, airborne ash and near masonry. A darker reuse of the vertical facade encloses the low approach, with piers extending below view; ascent uncovers the light above it. Later flying buttress roots extend beyond the lateral frame. An existing air layer shapes a stationary light shaft over the opening refuge; it adds no render pass. The facade replaces the former loaded arcade and preserves uninterrupted piers and transparent openings through its lower image boundary. Its far placement now exposes arch heads and capitals at the middle crossing, with a source-aware grade separating broad faces from recessed ribs. Landing, double-jump and wall-jump events create a small, smoothly arriving local gust for nearby ash and pinned cloth. Only the nearest ash plane responds; distant particles and all fog banks retain their independent ambient drift. Reset restores flock positions, expires that wake and clears lateral viewpoint drift. `wildlife.js` owns two flocks of 11 and 8 birds through `createFlocks()` (`update`, `draw`, `resize`, `reset`, `startle`). Checkpoint and summit bell events startle the flocks, which accelerate and regroup over about2.4 seconds. Their flight evolves on the presentation clock with separate weak parallax and a shared static 256×64 atlas containing 16 wing frames.

`foreground.js` exports `createForeground(assets)` with `draw(context, camera, height, viewpoint, time, reducedMotion, gust)`. Depth owns the instance and submits it through `drawNear`. Three complete silhouettes—an arch shoulder continuing into a pier, a clustered shaft and a broken buttress—are cached from the stone material once per asset identity; six authored placements share them and cull outside the viewport. Selected placements carry a folded pennant pinned to the pier, responding to presentation time and nearby gusts. Reduced motion fixes its wind pose and removes gust response. It introduces no frame loop or GPU image texture.

`atmosphere.js` exports `createAtmosphere(canvas)`, used by the world renderer with `#atmosphere-canvas` behind `#game-canvas`. It owns a WebGL default-framebuffer scenery compositor: one static quad buffer, one shader program, one precomputed cloud texture and at most six cached image textures under the one-time asset lifecycle. Those six are the graded background, far vertical facade, darker lower facade, belfry and middle pier plus the bird atlas; the cloud texture brings the total to seven. The darker lower facade adds one bounded cached texture and image submission inside the existing composition order, with no additional frame owner, air pass or offscreen target. `cloud-texture.js` exports seeded `createCloudPixels(size = 256)`, baking distinct body, billow and broad-variation fields into RGB with opaque alpha. The compositor generates its 256×256 texture and mipmaps at initialization or context restoration. The fog shader samples these fields three times per covered fragment for drifting cloud banks shaped by continuous ambient wind. Jump impulses do not change fog texture coordinates; the prior all-depth distortion made the distant banks snap and stretch. Image submissions accept an optional normalized crop for atlas frames.

`depth.js` owns the ordered image/air submissions under main's existing animation loop. There are no offscreen GPU render targets, per-frame image uploads or gameplay texture copies. The atmosphere buffer caps DPR at 1.5 and area at 1.1 million pixels; foreground gameplay remains capped at DPR2. Failed initialization or context loss uses the painted Canvas2D path; restoration rebuilds GPU resources. Reduced motion fixes scenery camera and shader time, stops flock flight/flapping and ash drift, removes nearest-ash gusts, segmented chain waves and camera impact, and reduces cage/bell excursions. It does not change simulation-owned contact motion. Renderer exposes read-only `atmosphereStatus` (`backend`, cached image `textures` excluding cloud noise, and drawing `buffer` dimensions) for validation; when no WebGL compositor exists, only `backend: 'canvas2d'` is returned.

`character.js` exports `createCharacter(assets)` with `update(game, dt, reducedMotion)`, `emit(events)`, `draw(context, game)`, and `reset()`. Renderer owns the instance and advances it once per rendered frame. Draw receives world coordinates after the camera transform and restores canvas state. Live body and mantle geometry uses a cached eyeless crop of `hero` as material, with eyes drawn separately. Landing recoil follows contact direction, with delayed ash shedding and bounded body compression; immediate takeoff exits the grounded recoil pose. Double jumps open gaps and disperse mantle pieces. A rear outer-mantle crescent and its two adjacent fibres remain absent while authoritative `player.airJumps` is zero. Grounded refill catches the core first, starts gathering existing pooled flakes after80ms and closes the missing section by360ms; immediate rebound retains valid recovery while another spend cancels stale refill flakes. Bell attention starts350ms after a resonant landing, with the crown following at470ms. Stillness at the opening refuge releases body/fibre tension through a short soft eye-close/exhale and relaxed half-open eyes. Pending bell attention permits120ms of initial landing deceleration; movement after that, leaving ground, death and restart interrupt attention immediately; poses never delay input. Body, eyes, fibres and bounded local effect pools remain presentation state. Main resets the renderer before restarting the game so a jump on the first restart frame retains its animation event. Paused frames use dt=0 for character, props, scenery and audio.

`surface-ash.js` exports `createSurfaceAsh()` with `emit(events)`, `update(game,dt,reducedMotion)`, `draw(context,game,cameraY)`, `reset()` and read-only `state`. It retains at most24 contact intents and16 contacts with14 grains each. Landing residue clips to its actual support and follows live suspension until individual grains release; braking sweeps it along the surface and immediate takeoff lifts recent residue. Wall-kick traces resolve the real solid face, including rib segments, and clip to it. Rendering invokes it after stone and before the character through the existing world transform. Reduced motion leaves a fading attached residue; pause freezes it and restart clears it. The legacy landing/ground-launch burst is removed; a few wall chips and delayed pin grit remain. `sceneryState.surfaceAsh` exposes copied inspection state only when requested.

`audio.js` exports `createAudio()`: returned object has `unlock()`, `setMuted(boolean)`, `emit(events)`, `update(game,dt)`, `dispose()`. All sound procedural Web Audio, activated only after gesture. Audio must not mutate game. Opening launches/low contacts are quieter; the suspended contact schedules lower iron, pin and low bell components through existing bounded audio voices. Incoming horizontal speed varies the existing contact scuff. A ground rebound within320ms on the same nearby surface shortens the four body gain envelopes, preserving the later iron and separate bell responses. One last-contact handle reuses those existing nodes; it adds no sources or timers. Visual pause freezes effects, while already scheduled audio tails retain their pre-existing AudioContext-clock behavior. Offline phrase WAVs exercise synthesis and cleanup, not headphone judgment.

`main.js` owns keyboard and simultaneous pointer input, modal focus, HUD, fixed-step scheduling, and event dispatch to renderer/audio. The development `window.__echo` surface provides inspection and harness hooks; informed scripted routes establish reachability, not first-play comprehension or human movement quality. The static directory is served locally on port 8317.
