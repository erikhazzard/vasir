# Revision17 landing baseline

The user's complaint reproduces in frozen revision16: medium and heavy contacts have nearly the same compact crouch and quick return. Most visible differentiation comes from debris and the suspended stone, rather than a substantially different response in the character's shape and timing.

## Method and material

`capture.mjs` freezes source bytes, serves them through scratch routes and records native390×844 normalRAF. It arranges starting height and zero velocity only, above the SAME `resonant-stone`. Gravity and the real collision/event pipeline produce every contact. No screenshot interrupts falling/contact/recovery. Real keyboard input just before contact exercises buffered rebound. The final three cases use the actual Gentle effects UI setting.

Baseline sources: revision16 `evidence/revision-16/source`; exact frozen/served hashes are in `baseline-v16/receipt.json`. All seven cases completed without page errors. Original normal-time video,25fps extraction and frozen source are retained. This is qualitative visual/causal evidence, not physical-phone performance or full-route proof.

## Required visual repair

The body itself needs a proportional timing ladder: a tiny settle, a clearly driven medium compression, and a distinctly heavier low compression with a brief weight-bearing hold and recovery. Eyes must stay inside a coherent head rather than floating above a pancake. The environment should answer the same severity through ash and chain/stone motion, not a separate arbitrary particle tier.

The medium case reaching almost the old ceiling helps explain why greater height does not feel materially different. A new distance/impact-speed severity contract is useful only if the visible shape and timing actually separate.

Preserve immediate buffered rebound, legible next ledge/hazards, Gentle-mode differentiation and covered camera edges. A long landing animation cannot lock controls. Increased fragment counts alone cannot satisfy this request.

No overall game letter grade or S-tier claim is assigned.
