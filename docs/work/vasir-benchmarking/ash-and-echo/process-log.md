## How to read the record

- **Human judgment** is the user's assessment of the experienced build. Record its scope: an overall grade, environmental art grade and character grade are not interchangeable.
- **Model judgment** is a bounded review opinion. Finding closure, relative improvement and successful traversal do not establish the requested artistic bar.
- **Runtime evidence** establishes only the behavior, source version and environment actually exercised. A source-informed route is not unprimed learning; desktop phone emulation is not physical-phone feel.
- **Kept/rejected** describes an implementation decision. It does not mean the user accepted that individual technique. Preserve failed attempts and reviewer disagreements when they explain the decision.
