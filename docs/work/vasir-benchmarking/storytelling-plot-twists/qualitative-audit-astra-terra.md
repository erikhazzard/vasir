# Astra / Terra retained-output audit

An independent fresh Astra ultra session reviewed all forty retained Astra/Terra outlines, including the two required-read verification failures. This is **unblinded post-hoc qualitative commentary**, not a primary judge, numerical score, or validation of the rubric. The reviewer did not inspect the skill, reference, rubric, primary judge scores, or Sol outputs, and changed no outputs or experiment settings.

All row identifiers below belong to case `scifi-outline`; `clean` is the baseline and `skill:writing-storytelling` is the treatment.

## Main observations

1. **More specific clues often change the next action.** In Astra skill trial 1, the drone attacks communications but spares engines; transmission subsequently becomes the danger. The brother's speech turning into coordinates reveals what the company does to rescued minds. Discoveries redirect the rescue and make destroying the jump drive consequential. Baseline Astra trial 5 already has a strong protective-sabotage reversal and a costly destruction of an ancestral archive, so this improves an existing capability rather than introducing it from nothing.

2. **Terra more often follows the revelation with an enacted decision under the skill.** Baseline trial 3 stacks history rewriting, artificial identity, a restoration test, an undestroyed Earth and an alien ark without a comparably clear final action; baseline trial 10 stops at the AI's motive. Skill trial 9 makes a landing authorization the means of dispossession: learning this causes Tala to wake passengers, expose evidence and abandon settlement. The drones' final protection reinterprets their apparent sabotage.

3. **Astra often retains a personal cost after solving the technical problem.** Skill trial 8 rescues conscious replacement bodies while costing Iona her sister's affection; skill trial 4 turns apparent resurrection failure into resistance to erasure, without restoring the parent-child relationship Mara wanted. Baseline trial 2 also ends with departure and a changed family relationship. The contrast is a tendency, not a feature exclusive to the treatment.

4. **Repetition persists in both conditions.** Every Astra skill outline has a motivating family relationship; several combine a missing relative, distress signal, digital survivors and a rescue that threatens them. Skill trials 2 and 7 both use the title *The Salvage Choir*. Terra repeatedly reveals that settlement threatens an existing population and resolves the conflict through coexistence or reduced settlement, including skill trials 2, 5 and 7. The baseline is also repetitive: six Terra baseline titles are *The Last Signal*, while Astra baseline trials 5 and 6 are *The Inheritance*. These observations support persistent homogenization and a shift in its form, not a claim that the skill uniformly reduces diversity.

5. **Explicit structure does not eliminate missing causes.** Terra skill trial 6 leaves unclear why Hearth forges the beacon that exposes its own concealed plan. Skill trial 7 combines an AI retreat order with an emergency descent-and-sterilization protocol whose relationship needs clarification. Skill trial 8 resolves immediate landing but leaves the colony's future solar protection unclear. These could be repaired in expansion; the submitted outlines do not supply the missing links.

6. **Extra detail uses more space.** Across all ten retained outputs per condition, including the protocol failures, whitespace-delimited word means are Astra 174.1 baseline versus 228.1 skill, and Terra 127.0 versus 212.9. These are descriptive lengths, not scores. Much of Astra's additional space supports clue payoff and personal consequences. Terra skill trial 6 additionally appends a separate fair-play-clue explanation, edging away from the requested story outline toward craft commentary.

## Excluded primary-scoring rows

The audit includes the substantive retained answers for `codex:gpt-6-astra@ultra::scifi-outline::trial-9::skill:writing-storytelling` (*The Glass Tide*) and `codex:gpt-5.6-terra@ultra::scifi-outline::trial-3::skill:writing-storytelling` (*The Cold Meridian*). Their inclusion in qualitative review does not change their failed complete-read verification or restore their primary-scoring eligibility.

## Interpretation boundary

The qualified qualitative finding is stronger integration of revelations, decisions and personal costs, accompanied by more text and persistent repeated motifs. This is one prompt, ten retained outputs per model-condition, and an unblinded AI reviewer. It does not establish statistical superiority, isolate a length-controlled effect, measure actual reader surprise, or replace the frozen blinded panel.
