# Sol / Luna retained-output audit

A separate fresh Astra ultra session read all forty retained Sol/Luna outlines. This is **unblinded post-hoc qualitative commentary**, not a primary judge or numerical score. The reviewer did not inspect the skill, reference, rubric, primary judging contents, or other models' outputs, and made no changes.

All identifiers below belong to case `scifi-outline`; `clean` is the baseline and `skill:writing-storytelling` is the treatment.

## Main observations

1. **The treatment adds development and length.** Whitespace-delimited word means, counting the entire answer including headings and Markdown markers, are Sol 138.4 baseline versus 235.6 skill, and Luna 164.6 versus 252.6. For each model, every treated output is longer than every baseline output. Both conditions remain short outlines, but extra foreshadowing and occasional thematic commentary represent a real brevity tradeoff, not automatic quality points.

2. **Sol's treatment shows more variety than its unusually repetitive baseline.** All ten baseline outputs are titled *The Last Signal*, repeatedly using distress calls, vanished colonies, simulations and AI survival tests. Baseline trial 9 adds another simulated layer and a failed-attempt number; trial 10 ends by announcing another experiment. These create more mysteries without much additional causal explanation. Treated stories use more distinguishable mechanisms, including memory archives, gate reconstruction, duplicated prisoners and geothermal resources, although identity, family loss and coercive protection remain recurrent.

3. **Sol's stronger treated revelations redirect action and pay off emotionally.** In skill trial 5, extracting the brother's data destabilizes a refugee; learning that he voluntarily surrendered his passage and that his remaining pattern sustains her converts rescue into a costly choice to delete him permanently. The ending substitutes telling someone about him for recovering him. In skill trial 2, a childhood lullaby changes from a lure into a memory colony to something a child freely teaches Lena after she relinquishes her recoverable past. The ending interprets the action instead of adding an unrelated twist.

4. **Luna makes agency clearer but repeatedly returns to the same answer.** Skill trials 9 and 10 reveal coercive protection, broadcast the truth and divide the future between physical existence and a collective mind. Their purposes are legible but similar. Skill trial 5 ends more distinctively with the brother-copy, stripped of command authority, asking permission to speak and waiting. Because Leena already states the importance of consent at the opening, that plot primarily tests her principle rather than changing her philosophy.

5. **Thematic clarity can leave the practical problem unsolved.** Luna skill trial 8 initially says landing will kill everyone, but later permits a merely dangerous landing without establishing what changed. Disclosure resolves authority more clearly than survival. Sol skill trial 7 plants specific clues to a nursery deception, but using parents' memory implants to run life support arrives late as the escape from its oxygen dilemma. Its emotional price is clearer than its technical preparation. Claims of fair planting do not establish it.

6. **Useful economy exists in the baseline.** Sol baseline trial 4 turns an apparent alien weapon into a shield and humanity's response to the warning into the threat, changing whether Mara should transmit. Luna baseline trial 2 uses a monument bearing the ship's name to anticipate repeated arrivals, then makes destroying the reset system follow from discovery; its final Earth-message hook is less integrated. Extra length, labeled beats or an explicit moral are not sufficient evidence of stronger storytelling.

## Interpretation boundary

These observations concern one prompt, ten outputs per model-condition and an unblinded AI reviewer. They suggest stronger causal and emotional integration in many treated outlines, while identifying additional length, repeated thematic solutions and unresolved mechanisms. They do not establish statistical superiority, a length-controlled effect, actual audience surprise or a substitute for the frozen blinded panel.
