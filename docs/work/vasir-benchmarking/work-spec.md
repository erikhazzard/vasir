# WORK SPEC: VASIRBENCH

**Purpose:** Build VasirBench, an author-specific multidisciplinary model-selection and workflow-regression lab that measures frontier models on the work the author actually uses AI for.
**User Journey Unlock:** An author can choose the best model and reasoning configuration for each recurring job family, compare a minimal baseline with an isolated skill and the normally routed full Vasir workflow, and see exactly where a model or workflow version helps, hurts, costs more, or fails.
**Engineering System Unlock:** Benchmark, treatment, model configuration, scoring, and evidence become independent axes, so the same task can compare skills, the complete workflow, models, reasoning settings, and later Vasir versions.
**vFinal:** From one local command, an author can run any bounded benchmark across an exact minimal baseline, one or more isolated skills, the normally routed full Vasir workflow, or named Vasir versions; chosen model and reasoning configurations; and repeated trials. The self-contained VasirBench lab exposes the complete matrix, exact inputs, outputs or artifacts, task-native checks, calibrated judgments, tokens, latency, and attributable cost. It produces a trustworthy model-routing profile across Engineering, Games, Product Design, Writing, and AI Workflows; reports absolute performance separately for every condition; shows matched help and harm; and answers whether a model release or Vasir edit improved the author's work. The system measures and exposes evidence; it does not present a benchmark score as proof of universal quality.
**Primary entrypoint(s):** `vasir eval run <benchmark> --treatment <skill:name|vasir> [--model <provider:model>] [--reasoning <effort>] [--holdout <path>] [--open]`, `vasir eval report <benchmark> [run-id] --open`, and from `VASIR-BENCH__M2`, `vasir eval calibrate <benchmark>`
**Reference routing:**
- [Implementation map](references/implementation-map.md): read §2–§6 before building the active rung; use §7 only when extending beyond response-only tasks.
- [Benchmark program](references/benchmark-program.md): read when classifying or authoring a benchmark, adding a condition or category rollup, designing AI Workflow evaluation, or qualifying a public claim.
- [Provenance](references/provenance.md): read only when prior eval decisions or the benchmark's origin affect a change.

## 1) North Star: vFinal

### 1.A Journey

- **Actor:** Vasir skill or workflow author.
- **Entry point:** Select an independent benchmark, one or more treatments, model/reasoning configurations, and trial count, then run `vasir eval run`.
- **Steps:**
  1. Vasir snapshots the benchmark, primary job family and tags, minimal baseline, declared treatments, model/reasoning configurations, scoring contract, and judge calibration.
  2. It runs the complete matched condition × model configuration × case × trial matrix and records successful, unavailable, and failed cells.
  3. The author opens the generated local lab in `Combined / Leaderboard`, sees the absolute condition-labeled cross-family model ranking and matched change for identical configurations, then selects any job family and moves between its Leaderboard, Benchmark tests, and Efficiency views without changing information hierarchies.
  4. The author drills into any cell to see the exact harness-controlled input, outputs or workspace artifacts, gate results, dimension scores, judge reasoning, and human verdict.
  5. The author adds a model configuration and rejudges the comparison cohort, tries the same benchmark against full Vasir, or edits a skill and reruns it.
- **Success:** Without opening raw JSON, the author can name the best eligible model/reasoning configuration for each measured job family and condition, explain how an isolated skill or full Vasir changed each contender, inspect every contributing benchmark, compare quality with cost and latency, and identify where coverage, calibration, or missing evidence makes the result provisional.
- **Next thing they will try:** Compare full Vasir, add another model/reasoning configuration and rejudge the shared cohort, or test an edited skill version on the same benchmark basis.

### 1.B Experience invariants: “it is not real unless…”

- `C-001`: The declared treatment is the only experimental difference.
- `C-002`: The exact experiment can be reconstructed from its artifact.
- `C-003`: The report exposes the whole matrix and its underlying evidence.
- `C-004`: Benchmarks own task prompts and scoring independently from treatments.
- `C-007`: New model results never silently mix with an incompatible experiment.
- `C-010`: Missing evidence stays visibly incomplete and cannot become a pass.
- `C-013`: A full-workflow result exercises normal Vasir routing rather than a hand-assembled prompt bundle.
- `C-014`: Benchmark scores remain measurements, not claims of proof.
- `C-015`: Every model and reasoning setting remains a distinct visible configuration.
- `C-016`: Prompt reports remain independent, and every category or cross-category score names its taxonomy, inputs, weights, calibration, and missing evidence.
- `C-017`: Model rankings keep every exact condition distinct while exposing matched change on one declared score basis.
- `C-018`: Every displayed condition names the exact intervention that ran; an isolated skill is never labeled full Vasir.
- `C-019`: Every benchmark has one primary job family determined by its judged terminal outcome; tags never double-count it.
- `C-020`: A scored panel is incomplete unless every required judge record is substantive and schema-valid for every candidate.
- `C-023`: Combined is the default capability and owns the cross-family leaderboard; Leaderboard, Benchmark tests, and Efficiency are peer views of the selected capability, with no redundant global lens layer.

### 1.C Obviousness audit

- **Assumption:** “Without the skill” means a genuinely clean comparison → **Implication:** both conditions receive the same useful neutral harness instructions and task; neither receives root steering, prior conversation, examples, or scoring criteria; treatment adds only its named context block at a fixed recorded insertion point.
- **Assumption:** A benchmark shows what was actually tried → **Implication:** exact prompts, context, settings, models, trials, failures, outputs, and score basis must be visible from the report.
- **Assumption:** “Any task” includes work that cannot be judged from prose alone → **Implication:** the harness standardizes experiments and evidence while suites supply task-specific gates, artifacts, and human review.
- **Assumption:** A higher average cannot excuse a fatal product miss → **Implication:** a missed defining-behavior or correctness gate caps the claim while execution and independent evidence continue.
- **Assumption:** A graph is evidence, not decoration → **Implication:** every aggregate must drill down to raw trials and have a table/text equivalent.
- **Assumption:** “The Vasir workflow” means the behavior produced by root steering, skill discovery, routing, tools, and agents together → **Implication:** the full-workflow treatment must enter through the same agent path the author normally uses and record what that path actually resolved and did.
- **Assumption:** A benchmark task should test more than the skill that currently owns it → **Implication:** benchmark identity, cases, and scoring must be selectable independently from clean, skill, and full-Vasir treatments.
- **Assumption:** Reasoning effort can materially change a model's result → **Implication:** provider, model, and reasoning setting form one visible configuration and are never collapsed into a model-family average.
- **Assumption:** Human calibration does not mean manually judging every run → **Implication:** the author calibrates task checks and LLM judges initially, then routine runs score automatically and expose disagreement.
- **Assumption:** Five headings represent five independent scientific faculties → **Implication:** they do not; they are provisional recurring job families used for model routing, with one primary family per benchmark and cross-cutting tags.
- **Assumption:** “With Vasir” describes any Vasir-authored context → **Implication:** isolated skill, full workflow, and workflow-version conditions remain separately named and separately scored.

### 1.D Design / UX bar

- **Experience target:** A credible local benchmark index that opens on Combined, identifies the strongest frontier models, then lets the author choose a job family and inspect its Leaderboard, Benchmark tests, or Efficiency in one stable workspace without making Vasir—or navigation chrome—the protagonist.
- **Reference bar:** The first architecture report in `VASIR-BENCH__M1`; that rung establishes the visual reference through human acceptance rather than copying a hosted benchmark product.
- **Must feel:** like a serious benchmark built around the author's real use cases: model performance first, paired Vasir effect second, category and task evidence immediately underneath.
- **Must not feel:** like Vasir marketing, a JSON viewer, opaque LLM vibes, a keyword scoreboard, or a decorative AI dashboard.
- **Human rejection criteria:** In three seconds the author cannot name the best eligible configurations, their exact conditions, and the measured scope; in thirty seconds they cannot see matched workflow changes, cost and latency, or enter a job family and benchmark; model/reasoning variants are collapsed; important failures are averaged away; or the lab implies proof it has not established.

## 2) Non-Goals

- No hosted service, account system, database, background queue, public leaderboard, or benchmark marketplace.
- No claim that automated judges establish universal correctness, taste, fun, or release readiness.
- No requirement that the first benchmark prove Vasir works; its job is to create a trustworthy measurement loop that can accumulate evidence.
- No arbitrary evaluator plugin framework in the active rung.
- No hardcoding the harness to architecture, chat, Redis/Valkey, games, or any other answer domain.
- No publishing private holdouts or committing generated run artifacts by default.
- No copying benchmark policy into every repository's `AGENTS.md` or `CLAUDE.md`.
- No replacement rewrite of the working eval CLI when its runner, provider adapters, and local history already provide the lasting base.

## 3) Request Anchor

- **Must:** Vasir is the author's personal opinionated AI workflow and includes a benchmark system that shows whether and how much it helps relative to a clean condition.
- **Must:** The report clearly shows the input prompts, every model/prompt/trial attempted, side-by-side outputs, scoring, judges, tokens/cost, and useful graphs.
- **Must:** Every job-family dimension exposes the exact benchmark suites and tests that compose it, and every benchmark row opens its individual evidence report while preserving the selected dimension as the return context.
- **Must:** The information architecture treats the cross-family leaderboard as `Combined / Leaderboard`. Combined and the five job families share one capability selector; Leaderboard, Benchmark tests, and Efficiency are the three peer views of that selected scope, with no higher-level Capabilities/Efficiency switcher.
- **Must:** Benchmark tasks and scoring are independent from the skill or workflow treatment so the same benchmark can test clean, individual skills, full Vasir, and later versions.
- **Must:** The comparison works both for a single skill and the complete Vasir workflow as an agent actually consumes it; manually concatenating preselected instructions is not an equivalent full-workflow test.
- **Must:** The target model matrix covers GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra, and Luna plus Claude Fable and Opus at each reasoning setting available to the author; every model/reasoning combination is a separate visible cell.
- **Must:** The lab shows which model configuration performs best under every exact condition, how the matched condition changes each configuration and job family, and whether a model or workflow edit improved the result.
- **Must:** Task-specific checks and LLM judges score routine runs after the author calibrates them initially; disagreements and uncalibrated dimensions remain visible.
- **Must:** The first real benchmark uses the prompt `Architect a chat app infrastructure that supports 10 million concurrent users.`, two independent fresh-context judge configurations (`codex:gpt-5.6-sol@ultra` and `claude:opus@max`), and separate fresh `codex:gpt-5.6-sol@ultra` synthesis sessions that produce the inspectable final scores and ranking from bounded matched batches.
- **Must:** The generated report uses D3 for a polished editorial data story and makes model ranking, complete answers, and Vasir lift immediately visible.
- **Must:** Multiple prompt reports are reachable from one generated local catalog without hiding incomplete attempts or merging unlike score bases.
- **Must:** The catalog is organized by an explicit versioned capability taxonomy and makes the category-level clean-versus-treatment result immediately visible before prompt detail.
- **Must:** The provisional top-level job families are Engineering, Games, Product Design, Writing, and AI Workflows. Every benchmark has exactly one primary family based on the terminal outcome being judged and may carry non-scoring cross-cutting tags.
- **Must:** Baseline, isolated-skill, normally routed full-Vasir, and workflow-version results remain distinct exact condition labels in artifacts, rankings, reports, and claims.
- **Must:** The primary product output is a quality, cost, latency, and failure-aware routing profile by job family. A cross-family default-model score requires declared personal workload and regret weights.
- **Must:** AI Workflow benchmarks score frozen steering artifacts through fresh executor agents on sealed downstream tasks, including negative transfer and invocation accuracy; the artifact's prose is never the terminal score.
- **Must:** The second real benchmark is a novel personalized-home-feed architecture task that is not described by a worked example in the treatment.
- **Must:** The third real benchmark is a high-volume device-telemetry architecture task with durable retries, monotonic latest state, expiring history, and no telemetry-specific worked example. Because general architecture rules were refined while discussing this case, it is development evidence rather than an untouched holdout.
- **Must:** The benchmark shape extends to novel architecture questions, stories and other creative responses, and workspace-producing tasks such as making a game.
- **Must:** Public regression cases and private rotating holdouts distinguish repeatable regression coverage from untuned generalization evidence.
- **Must Not:** Treat keyword matching, judge preference, or a single aggregate score as sufficient proof that an answer is good.
- **Must:** Within a declared job family, rank model/reasoning configurations separately for every exact eligible condition using a declared aggregation basis; show every benchmark's exact task score, cohort-relative contribution when used, task-family coverage, trials, uncertainty, and calibration state. A cross-family overall additionally requires explicit personal workload/regret weights and compatible calibrated contracts.
- **Must Not:** Average conditions together, silently drop a missing model/benchmark cell, or present an uncalibrated family or overall score as settled ground truth.
- **Must Not:** Mark a panel complete when any required judge returned a placeholder, empty/default record, omitted candidate, or otherwise non-substantive evaluation, even if synthesis can select another judge's record.
- **Must Not:** Leak hidden scoring criteria into the generated prompt or call a changed prompt/model/fixture a controlled skill comparison.
- **Preference:** Keep the product local, synchronous, inspectable, zero-runtime-dependency, and built on the current `vasir eval` path.
- **Permission:** Use suite-owned objective checks, semantic judges, artifact inspection, and explicit human verdicts in whatever combination the task genuinely requires.

## 5) Contracts & Invariants

- `[C-001 | Must]` Within every matched trial group, the model or agent runtime, reasoning setting, task and fixture, neutral base context, generation settings, output contract, tool permissions, and run environment are identical; the only assigned difference is condition. `clean` adds no Vasir steering, `skill:<name>` inserts one named skill snapshot, and `vasir` installs the complete workflow at its normal consumption boundary. Later routing, tool, or subagent differences are recorded treatment effects rather than preselected inputs.
- `[C-002 | Must]` Every run preserves the exact harness-controlled messages and context, treatment content or installation snapshot and hash, suite/rubric snapshot and hash, fixture identity, model or agent version and settings, observed routing/tool/subagent trace when applicable, every judge and synthesizer identity, their exact prompts and outputs, anonymized candidate/reviewer order and cohort hash, panel-evidence and final score bases, harness/scorer versions, usage, and failures needed to reconstruct what happened.
- `[C-003 | Must]` The local lab exposes absolute model/reasoning rankings for every exact condition, matched condition change for the same configuration, and comparable treatment-version movement without merging those questions. It shows the complete prompt × model configuration × condition × trial matrix, side-by-side outputs, gate and dimension results, judge reasons and disagreement, tokens, latency, and attributable cost; every chart drills into raw evidence and has a non-visual equivalent.
- `[C-004 | Must]` A benchmark independently identifies its cases and objective checks, semantic gates, anchored dimensions, artifact checks, or calibration questions, and can run unchanged against clean, any individual skill, and full Vasir. Evaluation-only criteria are never included in generation context, and treatments never own or rewrite the scoring contract.
- `[C-005 | Must Not]` A task-defined miss of defining behavior, correctness, safety, or day-1-to-vFinal topology continuity cannot be averaged into a positive verdict by prose quality, style, or other dimensions.
- `[C-006 | Must]` Every configured LLM judge produces one substantive schema-valid evaluation for every candidate in the same deterministic blinded batch plan; matched conditions stay in one batch and every call has explicit candidate, byte, concurrency, and timeout bounds. A separate synthesis authority may adjudicate anonymous reasons in fresh sessions, but any final numeric score must use one declared anchored scale shared across candidates rather than opportunistically selecting differently calibrated raw judge totals candidate by candidate. Candidate/model/condition and reviewer/model identity remain hidden at the appropriate stage, individual reasons and disagreement remain visible, and scores cannot support a positive claim until every required record completes and the panel-plus-synthesis behavior passes author calibration.
- `[C-007 | Must]` Scores may be ranked or compared only when the benchmark cases/fixtures, generation and trial basis, harness/scorer/rubric versions, configured judge set, cohort hash, batch policy and plan, panel evidence, and synthesizer basis match. Treatment snapshots remain distinct named versions and never merge as the same condition. Adding or replacing a judge changes scoring identity but not generation identity; `rescore` creates a new linked artifact from the saved outputs. Adding a candidate changes the cohort and batch plan, so it still requires rejudging the comparison cohort.
- `[C-008 | Must]` Public cases live with the independent benchmark as reproducible regression coverage; sealed local holdouts are visible in their local report but absent from the repository, and any holdout used to tune a treatment or scorer is retired into regression coverage and replaced before another generalization claim.
- `[C-009 | Must]` The author initially calibrates subjective gates, dimension anchors, independent judge behavior, and synthesis choices against a small labeled set. Routine runs use task checks and LLM judges automatically; uncalibrated dimensions, per-candidate panel disagreement, synthesis reasons, and any later human adjudication remain distinct evidence.
- `[C-010 | Failure]` If generation, any required judge record or batch, synthesis record or batch, or task evaluation is unavailable, placeholder/default, empty, omitted, partially parsed, or otherwise non-substantive, preserve every valid row and completed batch, mark affected cells `INCOMPLETE` or `NO SIGNAL`, exclude them from positive claims, and let the author recover by rerunning only incompatible or missing evaluation work. A failed panel or synthesizer never falls back to one judge, an average, or a partial score.
- `[C-011 | Must]` `run.json` is the sole durable evidence authority; `report.html` is a deterministic, regenerable, self-contained local projection that escapes untrusted model content and needs no server or runtime dependency.
- `[C-012 | Must]` A workspace-producing benchmark runs each condition from the same isolated fixture and records the resulting patch/artifacts plus task-owned executable and human evidence; a prose answer about the workspace never counts as task completion.
- `[C-013 | Must Not]` A full-workflow claim cannot come from concatenating a chosen set of root instructions and skills into a prompt. It requires the same supported agent entrypoint in identical clean and Vasir-installed fixtures, with the resolved root contracts, discovered or read skills, tool calls, and subagent activity captured as evidence.
- `[C-014 | Must Not]` A score, win rate, or calibrated judge preference cannot be labeled as proof that Vasir or a model is universally better; the report states benchmark coverage, calibration status, and the exact scope of every comparison.
- `[C-015 | Must]` Every provider, model, and reasoning-setting tuple is a separate configuration. Requested but unavailable or failed configurations remain visible as `UNAVAILABLE` or `INCOMPLETE` cells and never disappear from the denominator silently.
- `[C-016 | Must]` Each benchmark keeps its own task, rubric, run history, and report. The generated catalog is a disposable projection of authoritative `run.json` artifacts plus one versioned job-family taxonomy. A family rollup may aggregate only when the taxonomy explicitly maps every required benchmark, declares weights, identifies exact eligible complete runs and one exact condition snapshot, exposes calibration, task-family coverage, trials, and missingness, and yields `NO SIGNAL` for missing or incompatible evidence rather than silently reweighting it. A newer incomplete attempt remains visible even when the catalog links the newest eligible report as featured evidence. A cross-family overall remains absent until personal workload/regret weights and compatible calibrated score contracts are explicit.
- `[C-017 | Must]` Every job-family model leaderboard publishes a separate benchmark index for each exact eligible condition, matched change, benchmark and task-family coverage, calibration, and uncertainty for every provider/model/reasoning configuration. A peer-outrank index is labeled cohort-relative because the field changes its value. Exact task-local scores and paired deltas remain visible, conditions are never averaged together, and a configuration is withheld when any required mapped benchmark cell is missing or incompatible.
- `[C-018 | Must Not]` A report, catalog, artifact, or claim cannot label an isolated `skill:<name>` result as `vasir` or `With Vasir`. Every condition displays the exact treatment type, name, and version that ran; full-Vasir evidence requires C-013.
- `[C-019 | Must]` Every benchmark has one primary job family determined by the terminal outcome being judged: Engineering, Games, Product Design, Writing, or AI Workflows. Subtracks, downstream domains, proof types, and cross-cutting capabilities remain tags and never double-count the benchmark in family or overall scores.
- `[C-020 | Must]` A scored panel is complete only when every configured judge produced a substantive schema-valid evaluation for every assigned candidate and every required synthesis record is present. Transport success, valid JSON, an unselected placeholder, or another judge's complete record cannot satisfy the missing panel seat.
- `[C-021 | Must]` Each job family uses evidence native to its terminal outcome. Working software and systems require technical or executable evidence; Games require playable evidence and human feel where material; Product Design requires rendered journeys or decision evidence; Writing requires reader-facing artifacts and calibrated human judgment; AI Workflows require downstream fresh-agent lift on sealed tasks. A fluent prose proxy cannot replace the promised artifact or experience.
- `[C-022 | Must]` From every displayed job-family score, the author can reveal the exact benchmark suites and tests that compose it; each benchmark names its matched condition scores, completion and calibration state, and opens its individual report through a native link. Aggregate, benchmark, and report views preserve one visible category/benchmark hierarchy and one obvious return path.
- `[C-023 | Must]` The local lab opens on `Combined / Leaderboard`, where the cross-family paired model leaderboard is the Combined model view. Choosing Combined, Engineering, Games, Product Design, Writing, or AI Workflows changes the scope in place; Leaderboard, Benchmark tests, and Efficiency are the only peer views of that scope. Its configured panel is fresh `codex:gpt-5.6-sol@ultra` plus fresh `claude:opus@max`; separate fresh `codex:gpt-5.6-sol@ultra` sessions synthesize each bounded batch of anonymous judgments into final score records. - **Binding decision:** A single-skill treatment is an immutable context snapshot inserted at one declared point into an otherwise identical neutral prompt. A full-workflow treatment instead runs the same agent from an identical fixture with Vasir installed through its normal path and snapshots the context and behavior that agent actually resolves; a hand-assembled bundle is not equivalent evidence.
- **Binding decision:** User-visible condition names are `Minimal baseline`, the exact isolated skill name, `Full Vasir`, or an exact named Vasir version. An isolated skill result cannot be summarized as `With Vasir`.
- **Binding decision:** Keep one synchronous Node CLI and local filesystem history. The active path uses in-process benchmark/judge/report modules plus fresh Codex and Claude CLI subprocesses; no service, database, queue, or runtime frontend framework is forced.
- **Binding decision:** Public regression cases live with the independent benchmark; rotating holdouts stay local and sealed until use. Tuning a treatment or scorer consumes a holdout.
- **Binding decision:** Historical score comparability includes the candidate cohort, configured panel, each complete panel evaluation, and synthesizer basis. Changing judges requires a new score artifact but not new generations; adding a model or treatment output requires rejudging the combined cohort. `vasir eval rescore <benchmark> [run-id]` writes a new linked run and never overwrites the source evidence.
- **Binding decision:** `run.json` remains authoritative and `report.html` remains disposable, self-contained, and regenerable from it with vendored D3 through `vasir eval report`.
- **Binding decision:** Multi-prompt navigation is one disposable local `index.html` generated by scanning benchmark run artifacts and applying the checked-in versioned job-family taxonomy. It features the newest validity-eligible complete scored run per prompt while exposing newer incomplete or invalid attempts; it does not introduce a run-index database, service, or average of unlike prompt-local scores.
- **Binding decision:** The author-facing hierarchy is `job family → benchmark suite/track → benchmark → report evidence`. A family view may lead with its model leaderboard, but it must expose the exact constituent benchmark inventory as a peer view rather than burying it after every model row. Benchmark rows use the same paired-condition language as the leaderboard and navigate to a cohesive report route with a direct return to the selected family.
- **Binding decision:** The cross-family leaderboard is the `Combined` capability, not a peer product area. There is no global lens rail: the persistent capability selector chooses Combined or one of five job families, and one attached local rail chooses Leaderboard, Benchmark tests, or Efficiency for that scope. `#capabilities/overall` is the default landing; category Efficiency routes are `#capabilities/<category>/efficiency`, and the former `#leaderboard` and `#efficiency` routes remain compatibility redirects to `#capabilities/overall` and `#capabilities/overall/efficiency` respectively.
- **Binding decision:** The provisional portfolio taxonomy contains five top-level job families: Engineering, Games, Product Design, Writing, and AI Workflows. A benchmark receives exactly one primary family based on the terminal outcome being judged; `backend-architecture` is an Engineering track and other cross-cutting properties are non-scoring tags. The generated lab renders only families with real evidence. A family becomes rated only after heterogeneous task-family coverage, a sealed case, task-native human-calibrated scoring, complete evidence, and repeated close finalists. The author-facing default remains a routing profile by family; a cross-family overall additionally requires declared personal workload/regret weights and compatible score contracts.
- **Binding decision:** An AI Workflows benchmark freezes the created steering artifact and scores its effect through fresh executor agents on sealed downstream tasks, including false invocation, negative transfer, tokens, latency, and cost. The artifact's prose is not the terminal score.
- **Binding decision:** A visual performance workspace benchmark freezes a human-accepted look independently from its edit fixture. Generation comparisons give both conditions the same defect-free start; repair comparisons may give both the same declared, calibrated regression. In either shape, visual non-inferiority is a veto before topology or performance evidence can support a positive result.
