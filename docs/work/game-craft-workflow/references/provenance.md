# Provenance

The [Ash & Echo process log](../../vasir-benchmarking/ash-and-echo/process-log.md) records repeated creator corrections through revision 23. The creator accepted the final build as a good reference and explicitly approved converting the process into guidance for any game.

The most important prior rewrites are:

| Experienced failure | Replacement instinct | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| More assets/layers/effects still looked assembled or flat | Inspect relationships, shared material, occlusion, complete forms and meaningful composition changes | Art |
| A striking morph frame concealed static motion and paper-like remains | Judge the full material lifecycle and transitions into the next action | Juice |
| Repeated events looked identical despite different consequences | Derive response from meaningful state and event scale; preserve both quiet and strong accents | Juice |
| Ambient layers moved with input/camera and scenery felt disconnected | Separate environmental time from player response, and distinguish visual response from actual collision/state movement | Art and juice, at their respective boundaries |
| Optimistic agent judgments repeatedly missed what the creator experienced | Inspect representative real play, state one visible failure, repair its cause and compare against the previous build | Orchestrator |
| A local fix reappeared as the same failure in another emitter | Transfer the underlying failure mechanism across materials and states, not only the original asset | Owning specialist |

These are development-derived heuristics with boundaries. Ash, feathers, chains, fog, wall kicks and tall architecture are examples, not requirements. The accepted reference was extensively coached; it cannot itself establish autonomous transfer or workflow uplift.
