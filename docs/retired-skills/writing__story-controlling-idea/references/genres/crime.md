# Crime Genre Reference

## Value Spectrum — Operational Synthesis

The supplied source names the global poles. The deeper states below operationalize McKee’s contrary, contradictory, and negation-of-negation progression for this genre; tailor the exact wording to the story without leaving the canonical axis.

| Level | Genre state |
|---|---|
| Positive | Justice: wrongdoing is accurately exposed and proportionately answered |
| Contrary | Unfairness, uncertainty, procedural error, or partial truth |
| Contradictory | Injustice: the wrong person suffers, the criminal escapes, or truth cannot affect judgment |
| Negation of the negation | Tyranny or institutional injustice presented as justice; a system destroys its ability to distinguish truth from power |

## Core Roles — Source-Derived

- **Detective / Investigator:** Professional or amateur agent who follows the clues and exposes the criminal.
- **Criminal:** Perpetrator whose identity, method, motive, or degree of culpability is concealed.
- **Victim:** Person or group harmed by the crime; even apparently victimless crime must create victims.
- **Suspects:** Plausible alternative perpetrators who carry secrets, motives, and red herrings.

## Core-Emotion Techniques — Source-Derived

1. Clues and red herrings
2. Secrets and lies
3. Hidden motives
4. Suspense through uncertainty
5. Moral ambiguity around justice and punishment

## Genre Conventions — Source-Derived

- An antagonist MacGuffin or object of desire that explains the crime.
- Investigative red herrings that seem revelatory but mislead.
- Making it personal: the antagonist manipulates, threatens, or needs the investigator.
- A clock limiting the protagonist or antagonist.
- Subgenre-specific procedures, clue systems, and institutional constraints.

## Obligatory Moments — Source-Derived, Structurally Normalized

1. An inciting crime with real victims.
2. A speech or demonstration in praise of the criminal’s cunning.
3. Discovery and understanding of the antagonist’s MacGuffin or object of desire.
4. Progressively complicated following of clues, including red herrings and reversals.
5. Exposure of the Criminal: the core event.
6. Brought to Justice or Escapes Justice: the global resolution that establishes the final Justice/Injustice charge.

## Conscious Objects of Desire — Source-Derived / Consolidated

- Solve the crime and identify who is responsible
- Expose or bring the perpetrator to justice
- Exonerate the wrongly accused or oneself
- Commit the perfect crime and escape justice when told from the criminal’s perspective

## Genre Spine

The inciting crime disrupts safety and coherence. The investigator follows a causally connected chain of clues through deception and personal danger, learns the criminal’s object of desire, and exposes the criminal. The resolution determines whether exposure produces justice, incomplete justice, or institutional injustice.

## Twenty-Skeletal-Scene Map — Operational Normalization

The supplied notes often use “climax” for a decision, condition, or encounter. This map enforces:

- **Crisis = choice.**
- **Climax = enacted answer.**
- **Resolution = consequence.**

### Beginning Hook

| Command | Genre-specific function |
|---|---|
| Inciting Incident | A crime is committed or discovered. |
| Turning Point | A significant clue reveals that the apparent explanation is incomplete. |
| Crisis | Accept the convenient account or pursue the clue that creates risk and obligation. |
| Climax | The investigator commits to the case and chooses an investigative path. |
| Resolution | The first theory forms, suspects emerge, and the antagonist begins to react. |
### Middle Build One

| Command | Genre-specific function |
|---|---|
| Inciting Incident | New evidence connects the crime to a larger motive, victim set, or institution. |
| Turning Point | A persuasive red herring or personal manipulation redirects the investigation. |
| Crisis | Follow the institutionally safe lead or the dangerous anomaly. |
| Climax | The investigator acts on the chosen theory. |
| Resolution | A temporary success reveals a deeper contradiction and makes the case personal. |
### Middle Build Two

| Command | Genre-specific function |
|---|---|
| Inciting Incident | The antagonist’s MacGuffin and true object of desire become legible. |
| Turning Point | A major setback destroys evidence, discredits the investigator, or harms a victim. |
| Crisis | Protect position/safety or risk everything to pursue the true causal chain. |
| Climax | The investigator commits to the corrected theory and final exposure strategy. |
| Resolution | The last clues converge, but the cost and justice dilemma sharpen. |
### Ending Payoff

| Command | Genre-specific function |
|---|---|
| Inciting Incident | The final clue or contradiction makes exposure possible. |
| Turning Point | The criminal counters, implicates the investigator, or makes truth legally/socially unusable. |
| Crisis | Choose between incompatible forms of truth, safety, procedure, loyalty, punishment, or protection. |
| Climax | The investigator concretely exposes—or suppresses—the criminal and motive. |
| Resolution | The criminal is brought to justice or escapes it; the final state is Justice or Injustice regardless of whether the puzzle was solved. |

## Adjacent-Genre Disambiguation

- Choose Thriller when preventing further victimization and escaping a master villain’s personalized threat dominate the final Life/Damnation payoff.
- Truth/Deception is often the investigative means. It does not replace Justice/Injustice in the controlling idea when Crime is global.
- A criminal may escape punishment in a valid Crime story if the criminal is exposed and the resolution intentionally lands on Injustice.

## Controlling-Idea Value Rules

- The controlling idea must resolve Justice or Injustice: “Justice is restored…,” “Justice is denied…,” “Injustice prevails…,” etc.
- Truth, cleverness, procedure, courage, mercy, violence, loyalty, and institutional correction belong in the Cause.
- Exposure and punishment are distinct. Exposure pays off the core event; punishment or escape establishes the resolution charge.
