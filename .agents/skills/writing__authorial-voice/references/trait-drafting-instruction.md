## Instruction

Write original prose that fulfills the task and content contract using the chosen reference. User requirements override reference preferences. Follow explicit form requirements; otherwise apply relevant conditional form and response defaults in the reference, including sentence count, directness of delivery, and whether to demonstrate a thought or explain it. `Open` means unspecified, not a generic explanatory response. Resolve remaining choices from this task; these general instructions supply no default genre, narrator, tense, sentence length, or rhetorical form. Fulfill the task's substance, including requested explanations and practical guidance.

Read the whole reference before drafting. Understand its desired effects, priority order, conditional tradeoffs, and distinctions between governing principles and optional techniques. Apply those relationships to this task's form and register. Do not replace a substantive aesthetic with a generic checklist, require every device to appear, or convert examples into templates. Work with original aesthetics and shared craft; do not reconstruct a living author's distinctive voice from excerpts or a fingerprint.

Develop language, imagery, particulars, and the movement of ideas from the new content. Use any supplied independent choices. For decisions the user leaves open, choose what helps this piece realize the reference's priorities. Preserve the required effect at its appropriate scale; a short form and a sustained passage may need different realizations of the same aesthetic.

Where material is open, explore substantially different observations or situations before settling on phrasing. Different wordings of the same thought do not supply different discoveries. Use the reference's priorities to choose what merits development for this brief. Where content is fixed, work with relationships the supplied material supports. At consequential decisions, compare what alternatives accomplish and sacrifice. Review how the chosen material develops across the composition, including quieter passages and connective work. Retain the stronger choices without exposing working analysis unless requested. Do not manufacture alternatives for every sentence or make every unit equally emphatic.

Before returning the draft, review style adherence, form and constraint compliance, writing quality, and originality separately. A passage can visibly satisfy a technique and still fail to engage, clarify, persuade, amuse, or move as intended. Judge its actual effect in context. Check factual and logical continuity, permitted invention, perspective, and length against the contract. Inspect recognizable borrowed expression when source material is available; changing nouns in an existing image or sentence does not make it independent.

Correct demonstrated defects and return the completed draft according to the output contract. Keep working notes outside the prose. If a later revision is requested, preserve this draft separately when managing files. Do not repeatedly rewrite to maximize a judge score.
