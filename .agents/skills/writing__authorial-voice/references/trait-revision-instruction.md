## Instruction

Revise the draft to improve its own writing and its realization of the chosen aesthetic. User requirements override reference preferences. Preserve the original draft separately when managing files. Work with original aesthetics and shared craft; do not reconstruct a living author's distinctive voice from excerpts or a fingerprint.

Read the full reference and the whole draft. Recover the reference's desired effects, priority order, conditional tradeoffs, and distinctions between governing principles and optional techniques. Follow explicit form requirements; otherwise apply relevant conditional form and response defaults in the reference, including sentence count, directness of delivery, and whether to demonstrate a thought or explain it. `Open` means unspecified, not a generic explanatory response. Within the revision scope, the draft's incidental format may change. Fulfill the task's substance, including requested explanations and practical guidance. Resolve remaining choices from this task; these general instructions supply no default genre, narrator, tense, sentence length, or degree of ornament. Do not substitute a generic checklist or assume that every device should appear.

Establish what must survive and what the requested changes authorize. Preserve facts, claims, evidence, qualifications, events and causal order, character intentions and knowledge, relationships, perspective, and meaning unless the user asks to change them. Preserve intentional uncertainty. Do not invent evidence, motives, or events merely to enable a stylistic effect. If the draft conflicts with explicit requirements, follow the requirements and mention consequential corrections when the output contract permits.

Assess style adherence, form and constraint compliance, writing quality, and originality separately. Locate the draft's strongest effects and its consequential weaknesses in context. A technique's presence does not show that it works, and a high judge score does not prove quality. Ask what the passage achieves for this reader and task, where it loses force or clarity, and which reference priority should govern the repair.

Revise at the scale of the problem, from wording to whole-work movement. Preserve effective passages. Where plausible changes compete, compare their gains and losses against the reference's hierarchy, the content contract, and the original passage. Retain the original when the alternatives weaken it. Do not add conspicuous devices to display adherence or simplify away an intended effect without a reason grounded in the task and reference.

Keep wording, imagery, and idea development independently expressed. Replace recognizable borrowing with fresh expression that preserves required meaning. If source material is available, review possible borrowing separately from deciding how the prose should sound; absence of matching word runs cannot establish originality.

Compare the revision with the draft and content contract for factual drift, changed causality or logic, altered knowledge or perspective, lost qualifications, and length compliance. Repair demonstrated violations and finish this pass. Return only the revised artifact and any additional material requested by the output contract. Keep working analysis outside the creative text, and do not repeatedly rewrite to chase evaluator scores.
