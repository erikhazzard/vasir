# Romantasy

Use when the central relationship and fantasy conflict change each other's possibilities. This is the supplied guide's hybrid extension; pair it with [love](love.md) when the relationship's development needs closer design.

**Value:** love / isolation. **Core emotion:** wonder and longing. **Core event:** the love revelation—a recognition or commitment made consequential by the world's magical or political constraints. **Desire:** a relationship the lovers can actually choose and sustain within those constraints.

## Conventions and obligatory moments

- Establish the fantastical world through what it permits, forbids, or costs the lovers. Magic must affect their encounter and subsequent choices; scenery alone supplies no interaction between genres.
- Make attraction and deepening connection occur through shared difficulties, competing loyalties, and discoveries about this particular person.
- Let a revelation of power, identity, or allegiance change what trust requires. A betrayal or misunderstanding earns its place when established pressures produce it and its disclosure alters available choices.
- Bring personal and external stakes together in the sacrifice, confrontation, and love revelation. Declaring love should change an action, commitment, or cost.
- Register the resulting relationship and the world's remaining consequences. Fate can create proximity; the characters still need grounds to choose each other.

## Choose the fantasy pressure

| Source variant | Design consequence |
| --- | --- |
| High fantasy romance | World-scale duties and political allegiances complicate union. |
| Paranormal romance | Concealed nature or power changes vulnerability and trust. |
| Dystopian romance | Institutions regulate or punish attachment. |
| Mythological romance | Divine obligations, inherited stories, or prohibitions constrain choice. |

**Provisional controlling idea:** Love survives when the lovers disclose the curse binding them and surrender its power to compel devotion. The ending must demonstrate freely chosen attachment and the operative cost; a magical declaration alone cannot establish either.

Determine which promises the ending must fulfill. A fantasy victory can leave the relationship broken; that is a different outcome from the hopeful union promised by a romance. See [mixed genres](../genre-and-form.md#mixed-genres) for integrating both obligations.
