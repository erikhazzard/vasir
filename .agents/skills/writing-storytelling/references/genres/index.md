# Choose a genre guide

Choose by the central struggle and promised experience. A setting or sales category such as fantasy or young adult does not select a governing value. Read the matching guide, not this whole collection. For a hybrid, read both guides only when both promises govern the outcome.

| Genre | Starting value spectrum | Core event in the supplied guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Action](action.md) | Life / death | Hero at the villain's mercy |
| [Horror](horror.md) | Life / death / fate worse than death | Confrontation with evil |
| [Crime](crime.md) | Justice / injustice | Exposure of the criminal |
| [Love](love.md) | Love / hate | Proof of love |
| [Performance](performance.md) | Respect / shame | Big performance |
| [Western](western.md) | Freedom / subjugation | Big showdown |
| [Society](society.md) | Power / impotence | Revolution or contested transfer of power |
| [Thriller](thriller.md) | Life / damnation | Hero at the villain's mercy |
| [War](war.md) | Honor / dishonor | Big battle |

Two extensions accompany the genres: [Romantasy](romantasy.md), centered on love/isolation and a love revelation; and [MMORPG](mmorpg.md), rewritten around earned power, gradual stature, and local impact in a persistent world. These are workshop models, not additional canonical Story Grid categories.

## Apply the guide

Use the [Four Core Framework](https://storygrid.com/articles/four-core-framework) to connect need, value, reader emotion, and core event. Then derive the [controlling idea](../controlling-idea.md): **value + final charge + chief cause**. Genre supplies the initial value hypothesis; the actual ending and causal progression must earn the rest. A genre's core event need not be the story's final scene.

Conventions establish expectations; obligatory moments pay them off. Preserve their dramatic functions, adapting their embodiment and order to the work. The source's twenty-scene skeletons elaborate hook, two builds, and payoff; they are optional outlining scaffolds, not a quota for every request. Example controlling ideas are provisional design propositions, not theses to paste onto unrelated events.

Keep global, internal, and [scene-level values](https://storygrid.com/articles/value-shift-101) distinct. External victory can accompany moral loss. Refine the genre hypothesis when actual stakes contradict it. For internal arcs use [character arcs](../character-arcs.md); for medium, length, quiet work, and mixed promises use [genre and form](../genre-and-form.md).
