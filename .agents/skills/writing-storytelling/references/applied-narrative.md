# True and purpose-told narratives

Read when the user explicitly wants narrative craft for memoir, documentary, a factual account, a case study, or a brand story. Do not apply story machinery to ordinary instructions, reporting, or business communication that would work better directly.

## Determine the truth contract

Fiction permits invented events within its declared world. Memoir claims a relation to remembered experience. Documentary and factual case studies claim evidential accountability. An illustrative hypothetical can invent people and events if it is clearly presented as such. These contracts are not interchangeable merely because all can be engaging.

Before strengthening a true narrative, separate established facts, reported recollections, inferred motives, reconstructed language, and missing information. Do not fill an evidential gap with a dramatic invention and then present the invention as fact. If the user wants an imaginative reconstruction, preserve its status in the framing. A disclaimer in a work note does not repair a published passage that presents speculation as direct observation.

## Memoir: experience, evidence, interpretation

The younger self, the remembering self, other witnesses, and surviving records can disagree. That disagreement can be the story. It need not be corrected into one final omniscient explanation.

Suppose the narrator remembers a father missing a concert. A photograph later shows him outside the hall. The photograph changes the account of his presence; it does not establish why he stayed outside, whether he heard the performance, or what he felt. A responsible narrative can revise the narrator's judgment while preserving those unknowns.

Weak: “Only then did I understand that his love had kept him outside.”

Stronger, if supported by the supplied facts: “He was there. That was what the photograph gave me. It gave me nothing about the door.”

This is not a universal prescription for withholding. If a letter establishes his reason, use the evidence. If remembered speech is uncertain, shape it as recollection rather than a verbatim transcript. Literary force can come from a precisely bounded claim.

## Give a true account shape without inventing its cause

Select and order real material around a consequential question. Distinguish event chronology from the order in which a narrator or investigator learned it. A discovery can change a person's understanding now without causing the past event to happen now.

Compression should not create false causal adjacency. If layoffs followed a founder's speech but the decision had already been made, the speech did not cause the layoffs. If several people supplied a solution, a streamlined central character must not erase their contribution. A cleaner arc is not automatically a truer account.

A controlling idea in factual narrative is an interpretation answerable to the record. Test counterevidence and the limits of the claim. A case can illuminate how one institution failed without proving that all institutions of that kind must fail. Avoid inserting a moral transformation the subject did not undergo just to complete a preferred pattern.

## Purpose-told stories have an outcome outside the story

A commercial or organizational narrative has two distinct endpoints: what happens to the represented people, and what the audience is invited to understand or do afterward. Clarify audience, relevant need, and intended action before deciding how much story is useful. Enjoyment, comprehension, identification, association, intention, and behavior are different outcomes.

A negative phase can make competence legible: we see what had to be solved and why the solution matters. That does not require exaggerating fear or humiliating the customer. An organization that removes every difficulty from a success story often removes the evidence of its useful action. Conversely, a distressing story can overwhelm the modest benefit an offering actually provides.

Do not import a sales-story restriction into fiction. A clear, attractive next step may serve a product demonstration; a work of art can leave an irreducible dilemma. Likewise, art's satisfying ambiguity does not excuse an unclear or misleading product claim.

## Connect the offering honestly

Identify the actual causal role: necessary tool, enabling service, supporting convenience, occasion for gathering, or symbolic association. A product present at a happy ending did not necessarily produce the happiness. A campaign about community does not establish that buying is necessary for belonging.

The company's lived conduct is part of the narrative evidence. A promise of customer autonomy is undermined by a service that makes leaving difficult. Do not try to solve that contradiction with a more moving script. Name the mismatch and distinguish the product decision from the writing decision.

Avoid universal claims about brain chemistry, guaranteed persuasion, precise attention windows, or inevitable advertising obsolescence. Practitioner frameworks can suggest craft hypotheses; they do not establish those empirical claims. When current factual, medical, legal, or commercial assertions matter, verify them through the task's appropriate evidence process.

## Worked example: competence without a miracle

An invented inventory-service case starts with a shop whose owner promises same-day pickup before discovering that the display count includes damaged stock. A useful narrative lets the problem become specific: the customer has already arranged a ride, the employee did not create the inaccurate count, and the owner must decide how to respond.

If the supplied evidence shows that the service separates sellable from damaged stock and flags discrepancies, dramatize that mechanism. Do not claim the software restores customer trust instantly, eliminates all shortages, or changes the owner's character. The owner still has to apologize and decide what remedy to offer.

The inside-story result might be that a later order is promised accurately. The audience-facing invitation might be to inspect their own stock definitions or try the relevant feature. This is a narrower claim than “our product gives every family peace of mind,” and more useful because the demonstrated benefit has a real cause.

If there is no actual customer record, label the example hypothetical. If the requested artifact is merely a setup guide for the feature, give clear steps instead of imposing the shop drama on it.

## Verify the right kind of result

Inside the narrative, inspect causal coherence and the reader's understanding. Outside it, inspect evidence appropriate to the claimed effect. Views do not demonstrate persuasion; high conversion among self-selected readers does not isolate the story's incremental contribution; a before/after change can have other causes.

Propose observation proportionate to the task: can intended readers explain the actual benefit, identify the next step, and distinguish the claim from a broader promise? Business impact requires its own measurement design. Do not treat emotionally convincing prose as proof of efficacy, or declare that a campaign worked before it has been used.
