Based on the work spec, what is the goal in 1-2 lines? Then, in 1-2 lines each:
   *  What is the eng / system unlock? 
   * What is the user journey unlock? 

Then, in a clear 3-5 lines
 * What does "Done" ACTUALLY look like?

Remember, our goal is here to ground things in a high level user journey. From a code level, it's to ensure we're not building the wrong thing, and to ensure we're building the right thing the RIGHT way - no hacks, no 'split brain' shortcuts, no "v1" stubs.
