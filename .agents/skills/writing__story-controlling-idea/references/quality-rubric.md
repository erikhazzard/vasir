# Quality Rubric

## Hard Failures

Reject or revise any candidate when:

1. The Value is not one pole of the loaded global genre’s canonical axis.
2. The theme is only a word or topic.
3. The sentence contains several unrelated conclusions.
4. The Cause is merely an abstract virtue such as courage, truth, authenticity, unity, love, or faith.
5. The Cause is an unexplained metaphor such as shadows, light, monster within, destiny, or transcendence.
6. The crisis is mislabeled as the climax.
7. No concrete climactic action is stated.
8. No resolution is stated.
9. The resolution does not follow from the named action.
10. Luck, rescue, fate, or antagonist incompetence is the real cause.
11. The global genre’s core event or conscious object of desire is not paid off.
12. A secondary genre’s value replaces the global genre’s value.
13. The counter-idea is a straw man.
14. A third way avoids the crisis cost.
15. The negative ending is arbitrary rather than causally produced.
16. The philosophical attribution is unverified, decorative, inaccurate, or irrelevant.
17. The candidate could fit dozens of unrelated stories without meaningful change.
18. The candidate duplicates another candidate’s causal mechanism under new wording.
19. The output confuses depiction with moral endorsement.
20. The scope note contradicts rather than clarifies the controlling idea.
21. The resolution restores everything the climax supposedly sacrificed.
22. The genre was inferred from setting alone.

## Scoring: 0–5 Each, 50 Total

1. **Genre and value fidelity** — Correct global genre, canonical value axis, core event, object of desire, and obligatory-moment payoff.
2. **Causal specificity and agency** — Concrete agent, action, method, internal enabler, and consequence.
3. **Character-theme integration** — Want, need, fear, gift, flaw, arc, and climax are inseparable from the idea.
4. **Counter-idea strength** — Intelligent, effective, partially true opposition.
5. **Crisis and tradeoff quality** — Incompatible, reasonable, costly alternatives with residue.
6. **Climax-resolution proof** — Active answer and consequences actually turn the global value.
7. **Philosophical fidelity** — Accurate, relevant, source-grounded, and behaviorally embodied.
8. **Earned novelty or irony** — New mechanism or structural inversion, not verbal novelty.
9. **Clarity and singularity** — One crisp Value + Cause sentence understandable without jargon.
10. **Buildability and portfolio distinctness** — Coherent story can be built; candidate is materially different from others.

## Output Threshold

Return a candidate only when:

- Total score is at least **40/50**.
- No category scores below **3**.
- Genre/value fidelity, causal specificity, and climax-resolution proof each score at least **4**.
- No hard failure remains.

## Portfolio Audit

Across candidates, vary:

- Cause.
- Protagonist strategy.
- Antagonist theory.
- Crisis type.
- Tradeoff.
- Philosophical tradition.
- Irony mechanism.
- Mixed charge pattern.
- Subgenre implementation.

Ten paraphrases of “freedom comes through surrender” count as one idea.
